Summary: Docs Office is designed to work offline on your device. Your documents stay on your phone. We do not operate a cloud document-conversion service, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how Docs Office (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Android application published on Google Play. Replace the contact email above with your support address before publishing.

2. Information the App processes

Depending on how you use the App, the following categories may be processed:

  • Documents and files on your device. The App indexes and opens documents you choose to access (for example PDFs, images, Office files, and scans) using Android storage permissions, including all-files access where granted, so it can build your local library from folders such as Downloads and messaging apps.
  • Camera images. If you use document scanning, the App uses the camera to capture page images, crop them on-device, and generate a local PDF.
  • App preferences. Settings such as theme, excluded folders, reader preferences, and whether crash reporting is enabled are stored locally on your device.
  • Vault data. Items you move into the private vault are encrypted on the device. Unlock may use your device PIN/passcode flow and/or biometric authentication provided by Android.
  • Optional diagnostics. If you enable analytics/crash reporting in Settings, the App may send crash and diagnostic data to Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to help us fix bugs. This is off by default.
  • Purchase data (if subscriptions are offered). Google Play processes payments. We do not receive your full payment card details.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not upload your documents to our servers for viewing, conversion, OCR, or storage as part of core App features.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your document contents for advertising.

4. Permissions

The App may request:

  • Storage / all files access — to discover and manage documents in your library.
  • Camera — for the document scanner.
  • Notifications — for optional background scan or maintenance notices.
  • Biometric / device credential — to unlock the private vault.
  • Internet — only as needed for optional crash reporting and Google Play Billing, not for core offline document workflows.

5. How we use information

  • To provide local library, viewer, search, scan-to-PDF, PDF tools, and vault features.
  • To remember preferences and recent searches on your device.
  • If you opt in, to diagnose crashes and improve stability via Firebase Crashlytics.
  • To process Play purchases if a subscription or in-app product is available.

6. Sharing

Document contents are not shared with us for cloud processing. If crash reporting is enabled, diagnostic data is processed by Google Firebase under Google’s terms and privacy policy. Google Play processes purchase transactions. We do not sell data to data brokers.

7. Retention

Documents and vault items remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Local preferences are removed when you clear App data or uninstall. Opt-in Crashlytics data is retained according to Google Firebase retention practices.

8. Security

Vault items are encrypted on-device. No security measure is perfect; protect your device with a lock screen and keep the App updated.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke storage, camera, notification, or biometric permissions in Android Settings.
  • Keep crash reporting disabled (default), or turn it off later in App Settings.
  • Delete scans, library entries, or vault items within the App, or clear App storage / uninstall.

11. International users

Processing for core features happens on your device. If you enable Crashlytics, Google may process diagnostic data in locations where Google operates.

12. Changes

We may update this policy when features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be revised. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
